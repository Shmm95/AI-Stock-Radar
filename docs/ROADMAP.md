# Approved Development Roadmap

Development must proceed in this order:

1. Deterministic research foundation.
2. Portfolio validation.
3. Trade Timing Attribution and entry diagnostics.
4. Execution and slippage stress testing.
5. Research baseline lock.
6. Broader-universe robustness.
7. Paper-trading architecture and validation.
8. Long-duration paper validation.
9. Small-capital manual-approval live trading.
10. Controlled automation.

Each stage is a gate for the next. Existing code or artifacts from a later stage do not constitute approval to advance the pathway.

Paper trading must not be moved ahead of the research pathway. Real-money execution and controlled automation require separate, explicit authorization at their respective stages.
